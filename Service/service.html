Our Core Services

Special Purpose Machines (SPMs)

Teknohub Engineering Solutions designs and manufactures customized Special Purpose Machines (SPMs) to meet specific industrial manufacturing requirements. Our machines are developed to improve production speed, accuracy, consistency and operational efficiency while reducing manual effort and material wastage. We provide specialized solutions for feeding, cutting, crimping, trimming, drilling, tapping, milling, boring, grinding and hydraulic, pneumatic and hydro-pneumatic pressing applications.

Test Rigs and Inspection Systems

We develop advanced test rigs and inspection systems to ensure product quality, accuracy and reliability throughout the manufacturing process. Our solutions include leak testing, flatness testing, torque testing and nut runner inspection systems. These systems help manufacturers identify defects, maintain consistent product standards, reduce manual inspection and improve overall production quality through accurate and repeatable testing.

Design, Engineering and R&D

Our design, engineering and research and development services focus on transforming industrial requirements into practical and innovative engineering solutions. We provide product development, prototyping, plant layout design and customized mechanical and electronic engineering solutions. Using SolidWorks for mechanical design and advanced controller development platforms such as ATmega328P, STM32 and ARM Cortex, we develop efficient solutions that meet specific industrial and manufacturing needs.

Welding Automation

We offer specialized welding automation solutions designed to improve welding accuracy, productivity and operational efficiency across different industrial applications. Our product range includes welding positioners with capacities from 25 kg to 5,000 kg, rotators ranging from 1 to 150 tonnes, welding column and boom systems, beam welding automation and customized welding SPMs. These solutions provide better workpiece positioning, controlled rotation and improved accessibility, helping achieve consistent welding quality and reduced manual handling.

Control Systems and Automation

We provide comprehensive industrial control systems and automation solutions to improve machine performance, productivity and operational safety. Our expertise includes PLC, HMI, VFD, SCADA and LabVIEW-based automation systems using leading industrial brands such as Allen-Bradley, Siemens, Mitsubishi, Delta, Keyence and National Instruments. Our automation solutions help monitor processes, control equipment, minimize operator errors and ensure smooth and consistent industrial operations.

Material Handling Systems

We design and manufacture customized material handling systems to ensure smooth, safe and efficient movement of materials across industrial production facilities. Our solutions include overhead conveyors, stainless steel screw feeder conveyors, pick-and-place systems, indexing systems, crate trolleys, monorail systems and automatic transfer systems. These systems help reduce manual handling, optimize floor space, improve material flow and maintain continuous movement between different stages of production.
